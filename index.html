What Are Hunter Jumper Horses?

Hunter jumper is the umbrella term for the two primary English riding disciplines competed at USEF-rated horse shows across North America and internationally — hunters and jumpers. Hunter horses are judged subjectively on their movement, jump style, and manners, while jumper horses are judged objectively on faults and time. Equitation is a related discipline judged on the rider rather than the horse. Hunter jumper horses range from beginner-safe children's horses and short stirrup ponies through Grand Prix show jumpers and USHJA International Hunter Derby horses — making it one of the most diverse and competitive equestrian disciplines in the world.
